🎓 PhD Thesis Overview
2020 - 2024

Thesis Abstract

This academic study examines the effectiveness of the inoculation theory in combating misinformation and science denial, particularly with a focus on climate change. The research compares active and passive inoculation methods to help individuals build resilience against disinformation. The process highlights the Turkish adaptation and educational use of a digital game called “Huysuz Dayı” (Cranky Uncle), which teaches participants to identify logical fallacies and sophistry. Findings show that both methods improve the ability to defend scientific facts, but gamification makes the learning process more enjoyable and memorable. Furthermore, the text provides a comprehensive framework for identifying rhetorical techniques such as fake experts and conspiracy theories. Ultimately, the study emphasizes the critical importance of developing critical thinking habits in the digital media age for societal health and scientific awareness.

🎓 Master's Thesis Overview
2016 - 2019

Thesis Abstract

This academic study is a master's thesis that examines the relationship between multiculturalism and education through the lens of Turkey's social structure and linguistic diversity. Prepared by Çağdaş Çetinkaya, the work addresses the distribution of ethnic identities in Turkey from a social sciences perspective while also discussing the effects of language development on cognitive processes. The text presents concrete data, particularly on the ways in which the people of the Akçakale region use Turkish and Arabic together and on local dialect differences. In addition, the appendices of the study include astronomy-themed lesson plans and achievement tests designed to measure students' gains in science. The source demonstrates an interdisciplinary approach covering both cultural anthropology and educational methodology. In this context, the author has created a comprehensive academic framework by supporting theoretical knowledge with practical educational materials and statistical analysis methods.
