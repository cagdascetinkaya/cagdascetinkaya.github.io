Trained in the halls of Boğaziçi, Akdeniz and Mersin Universities, Çağdaş Çetinkaya is a science educator who studies how minds learn to resist misinformation—and then builds the tools to prove it. Armed with inoculation theory, misconception-based teaching and a quiver of statistical methods, Çağdaş now crafts interactive, data-driven web applications that turn classrooms into living laboratories, combining academic discipline with the creativity of the coding world.
