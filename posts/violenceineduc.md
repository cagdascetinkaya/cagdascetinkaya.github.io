### **DECLARATION ON THE SYSTEMIC CRISIS OF EDUCATOR SAFETY AND PROFESSIONAL DIGNITY**

**To the Public, (Wise) Policymakers, and the Educational Community:**

*(even if it won't be read, or even if it is read, it won't be understood)*

We stand at a critical juncture where the sanctity of our educational institutions has been violently shattered. The escalating wave of aggression against educators in Türkiye is no longer a series of isolated incidents; it is a systemic crisis that threatens the very foundation of our education system and our society.

**1\. The Unacceptable Reality of Fatal Violence** Schools, which must be the absolute safest environments for both intellectual growth and physical well-being, have increasingly become sites of lethal vulnerability. The timeline of recent tragedies paints a devastating picture:

* The tragic assassination of Principal **İbrahim Oktugan (May 2024\)** by a former student marked a definitive crossing of the threshold into armed, fatal violence in our schools.  
* The murder of School Counselor **Muhammed Öz (June 2025\)**, who was killed with a firearm smuggled seamlessly into the building, exposed catastrophic and undeniable security failures.  
* The premeditated stabbing of Biology Teacher **Fatma Nur Çelik (March 2026\)** in her own classroom demonstrated that basic perimeter security and protective protocols remain practically non-existent.

**2\. The Erosion of Societal Respect and Authority** This physical violence is the direct, tragic manifestation of a prolonged ideological and administrative devaluation of the teaching profession.

* **The "Customer-Service" Paradigm:** The societal perception of the educator has been systematically downgraded from a respected community leader to a mere service provider. Unregulated, disproportionate, and easily abused complaint mechanisms have heavily empowered parents and students, fostering a toxic culture of entitlement.  
* **A Culture of Impunity:** The historical lack of immediate, severe, and deterrent legal consequences for threats and physical assaults has emboldened perpetrators. It has created a dangerous environment where an educator's safety is compromised without hesitation.

**3\. The Imperative for Unified Action** The time for fragmented, ideologically divided responses has passed. The survival and well-being of our educators require absolute, uncompromising solidarity across all unions and institutions. We urgently demand:

* **Immediate Security Infrastructure:** The mandatory installation of metal detectors and the permanent assignment of professionally trained security personnel at the entrances of all educational institutions.  
* **Zero-Tolerance Legal Framework:** The immediate enforcement of strict legislative measures that categorize any physical or verbal assault on an educational worker as a major, non-bailable offense with severe, unmitigated penalties.  
* **Restoration of Professional Dignity:** A systemic overhaul of the administrative approach, officially prioritizing the protection, authority, and professional autonomy of the educator over appeasing baseless grievances.

All in all, an education system that cannot protect its teachers cannot educate its youth. We categorically refuse to accept violence as an occupational hazard. We demand immediate, structural reform to restore safety, dignity, and respect to our schools before another life is lost.
